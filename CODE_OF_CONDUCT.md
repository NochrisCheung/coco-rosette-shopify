# Coco Rosette Code of Conduct

This repository supports respectful, focused collaboration on the Coco Rosette storefront.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in reviews, discussions, and hand-offs.
- Discuss design, code, and decisions rather than making personal remarks.
- Protect merchant, customer, and contributor privacy. Do not share credentials, personal addresses, contact details, order data, screenshots containing private data, or other sensitive information.
- Raise concerns early when a request creates a safety, legal, accessibility, security, or customer-experience risk.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, or deliberate disruption.
- Sharing private information without the owner’s permission.
- Introducing secrets into source code, documentation, issues, logs, screenshots, or commits.
- Making an unapproved live-theme, Shopify-admin, financial, legal, or external-service change.

## Handling concerns

Raise a concern privately with the project maintainer through the agreed project communication channel. Maintainers should assess the concern promptly, limit disclosure to those who need to know, and take proportionate corrective action. No external Shopify or third-party support address is a reporting channel for this repository.
